# Prototype assets

All inventory, prices and stock are fictional. Photos are representative illustrations of the browsing layout, not images of cars offered for sale. Color, model year and trim are mock option data and may differ from the representative photos. Used-vehicle photography must be replaced with the actual physical vehicle before production.

Downloaded from Unsplash's public image CDN, resized to 1400px wide at quality 85. The [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license) permits free commercial and noncommercial use. No images were copied from the three reference dealerships. Source identifiers are retained here for provenance.
